Personalisation & Packaging

Every gift is hand-finished in our atelier before it leaves the cellar.

01

Satin Ribbon Tying

Each ballotin is hand-tied with rich double-faced satin ribbon in gold, bronze or custom brand colours.

02

Gold-Foil Embossing

Boxes are embossed with our estate crest or your company logo in metallic gold foil for a premium finish.

03

Handwritten Cards

Every gift includes a hand-written message card in gold-foil script, supplied by our concierge team.

04

Chilled Protective Packing

Every parcel travels in insulated liners with food-grade gel packs to keep it between 15°C and 18°C.
