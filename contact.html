Quick Answers

Contact & Shipping FAQ

Instant answers regarding response times, order modifications, and showroom policies.

01 What is the average response time for enquiry submissions?

Our concierge team reviews all digital form submissions within 2 hours during boutique operating hours. For urgent corporate orders, we recommend calling our toll-free line directly.

02 Can I modify or add items to an existing corporate gift order?

Modifications can be made up to 48 hours prior to scheduled air-freight dispatch. Please contact your assigned corporate account manager directly or email concierge@cacaovelvet.com.

03 Are walk-in tastings available at our New York flagship boutique?

Walk-ins are welcome for boutique purchases and counter tastings. However, private VIP lounge consultations require a 24-hour advance reservation to ensure sommelier availability.
