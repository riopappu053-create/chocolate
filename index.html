About Cacao & Velvet

Our Passion for Uncompromising Quality

Founded with a singular vision to bridge the world's most elite chocolate makers with discerning connoisseurs, Cacao & Velvet curates rare, single-origin cacao bars and artisanal confectionery from legendary European and South American estates.

"Chocolate is not merely a treat; it is an exploration of terroir, culture, and centuries of artisanal mastery."

Every selection in our store is air-freighted under temperature-controlled conditions to preserve delicate flavor profiles, ensuring that when you open a box from us, it tastes as pristine as the day it was crafted in Zurich, Brussels, or Quito.

Client Assistance

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our international import standards, temperature-controlled shipping, and custom orders.

01 How are imported chocolates shipped to ensure freshness?

All our chocolates are air-freighted directly from estate kitchens in Europe and South America. During transit and local delivery, we use specialized temperature-controlled packaging and ice gel packs to guarantee pristine melt-free arrival.

02 Can I customize corporate gifting packages with company branding?

Yes! We specialize in bespoke corporate executive gifting. We offer custom laser-engraved wooden presentation boxes, personalized gift inserts, and custom ribbon branding for your clients and team members.

03 Are your chocolates certified organic and single-origin?

We partner exclusively with certified estates that practice ethical, sustainable harvesting. Many of our bars and truffles feature single-origin Arriba Nacional and Criollo cacao beans with verified traceability.

04 What is the recommended storage temperature for luxury confections?

For optimal flavor and texture, store your fine chocolates in a cool, dry place between 15°C and 18°C (59°F to 64°F) away from direct sunlight and humidity.
