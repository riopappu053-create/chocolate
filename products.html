The Cacao & Velvet Standard

Why Our Products Travel Better

Four guarantees behind every bar, truffle and ballotin that leaves our cellar.

Air-Freight Only

No sea transit, no warehousing delays. Every order flies direct from the estate kitchen to our climate-controlled cellar.

Cold-Chain Packing

Insulated liners and food-grade gel packs keep every parcel between 15°C and 18°C, even in peak summer.

Ethical Estates

We source only from certified estates practising sustainable harvesting and verified cacao traceability.

Melt-Free Promise

If a parcel arrives damaged, we replace it or refund in full — no lengthy claims process required.

Bespoke Curation

Can't Find Your Estate?

Our chocolate sommeliers source rare allocations on request — from vintage Valrhona harvests to limited Criollo releases.
