Foundational Philosophy

Bridging Legendary Estates with Connoisseurs

Cacao & Velvet was born from an uncompromising passion for authentic international confectionery. Founded by master chocolatiers who journeyed across the fertile mountain valleys of Switzerland, Belgium, Ecuador, and Venezuela, our mission is to bring rare single-origin expressions directly to your table.

We believe that true luxury chocolate is an immersive sensory experience—reflecting the rich mineral soils, seasonal climate, and artisanal heritage of its origin.

Specialized Air-Freight & Thermal Protection
Logistical Precision

Preserving Delicate Profiles Through Air Freight

Fine chocolates are extraordinarily sensitive to temperature fluctuations and humidity. Standard cargo shipping can degrade the delicate cocoa butter crystal structures and mute subtle floral or fruit undertones.

That is why Cacao & Velvet exclusively utilizes temperature-regulated air freight from European and South American ateliers directly to our New York flagship showroom. Every shipment is sealed in insulated thermal packaging with calibrated gel ice packs, guaranteeing absolute pristine condition upon arrival.

The non-negotiable benchmarks that govern every product we import and offer.

Purity of Ingredients

Zero artificial preservatives, emulsifiers, or hydrogenated oils. Pure cocoa butter, organic cane sugar, and real vanilla bean extract only.
