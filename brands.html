Centuries of Craft

European Confectionery Legacies

Key historical innovations pioneered by the partner houses available in our flagship store.

1857

The Brussels Praline

Introduction of filled chocolate shells in Belgium, transforming solid cacao into delicate multi-textured confections.

1922

French Grand Cru

Application of fine wine terroir classification to single-estate cocoa harvests in the Rhône Valley.

1932

Champagne Truffle

First blending of vintage French Champagne with fresh Swiss alpine cream inside a dark chocolate core.

2002

Tree-to-Bar Origin

Full fermentation, roasting, and bar molding performed at the country of cacao origin in Ecuador.
