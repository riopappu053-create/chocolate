Connoisseur Guide

Storage, Freshness & Care

Fine chocolate behaves like fine wine. Follow these four principles to protect aroma, snap, and shine.

01

Ideal Temperature

Store between 15°C and 18°C (59°F–64°F) in a cool, dry place, away from radiators, ovens, and direct sunlight.

02

Avoid the Fridge

Refrigeration introduces moisture and odours that cause sugar bloom. If you must chill, seal airtight and let the box reach room temperature before opening.

03

Keep Airtight

Cocoa butter absorbs surrounding aromas. Reseal opened ballotins in an airtight container and keep them away from spices, coffee, and strong cheeses.

04

Shelf Life

Dark bars keep 12 months; milk and white chocolate 6–9 months; fresh cream truffles are best enjoyed within 3–4 weeks of delivery.

Sommelier's Serving Tip

Remove your chocolates from storage 15–20 minutes before serving. At room temperature the cocoa butter softens, releasing the delicate floral and fruity notes of single-origin cacao.
